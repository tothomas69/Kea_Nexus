What do Kea, ARP, Pi-hole and Fingerprint mean?
Kea
Adds a DHCP deny rule for the device's MAC, so it can't renew or get a new lease. On its own this doesn't drop a live connection; the device keeps working until its lease runs out, which is why the other steps run too.
ARP
A background loop tells the device its gateway lives at a black-hole MAC address. This is what cuts a live connection right away.
Pi-hole
Blocks every DNS lookup from the device's address, on both Pi-holes, so a connection that survives the ARP step still can't resolve a name.
Fingerprint
Refreshes the device's OS fingerprint with an nmap scan. It runs on Quarantine and Release alike: it keeps identity current and is not part of enforcement, so a failed scan doesn't mean the action failed.
